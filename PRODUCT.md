# EcoRoute AI
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Delhi travelers comparing route duration and estimated pollution exposure; hackathon evaluators.
The user confirmed a city dashboard first, with route planning in a side panel.

## Product Purpose
Compare genuine road routes using provenance-aware environmental data and concentration-time exposure.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing FastAPI/Cognito REST contract is authoritative. AWS deployment, physical wearable support
and measured-data model training remain unverified. Never invent live readings or exposure reduction.
Frontend is now explicitly authorized, superseding the previous backend-only scope.

## Brand Commitments
EcoRoute AI identity. User-supplied Axle screenshot/video are visual references: cinematic 3D city,
charcoal left metrics, silver surroundings, orange accents, rounded navigation and floating controls.
Do not reuse Axle branding or its unsupported statistics.

## Evidence on Hand
examples/delhi-recorded-response.json: actual historical OSRM/Open-Meteo comparison; no demonstrated
exposure improvement. The city architecture is illustrative, not a factual map of Delhi.

## Product Principles
Keep provenance visible. Missing is not zero. Protect tokens and personal data. Preserve backend APIs.
